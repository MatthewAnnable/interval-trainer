How it works

  1. The app plays one note, the root, and shows it on the fretboard.
  2. It shows an interval, like "Play the Major 3rd". Play that note on your guitar, above the root.
  3. It listens. When you get it, it moves on to the next one by itself, so you can keep your hands on the guitar.
